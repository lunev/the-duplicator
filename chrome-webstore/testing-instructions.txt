1. Install and pin the extension icon in the Chrome toolbar.
2. Open any tab, e.g. example.com.
3. Click the extension icon, or press Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+L, to open the popup.
4. Type a URL parameter (e.g. admin) and press Enter or click Add.
5. Click the new parameter link, or press Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+L then 1, to open a duplicate tab with that parameter appended to the URL.
